## Full description
```
Foreign is the app for students who moved away from home to study: get to know your new city, get answers and build a community.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Community feed
Share posts with text and images, like and comment on what other students post.

Questions and answers forum
Ask what you need to know (where to rent, how to get around, paperwork, life in the city) and answer to help the ones who arrive after you. You can like and comment on answers.

Recommendations map
Discover and add places: universities, restaurants, housing, entertainment, transport and more. Rate places, add photos and search for spots near you. The map uses your location only while the app is open, and you can use the app without granting the permission.

Mentors
Meet mentors who have been through the same thing, check their topics and availability and message them in chat.

Friends and chat
Send friend requests, talk in direct messages and get notified when someone writes to you.

Rides between friends
Ask the friends you choose for a ride: pick the destination on the map, they get a notification and, once someone accepts, you see their route.

Notifications
Stay on top of comments, messages, friend requests and other activity, inside the app and with optional push notifications.

Your profile
Add your name, university, hometown, interests and a profile photo. Sign in with email, Google or Apple.

A SAFER COMMUNITY

- Report posts, comments, messages or users that break the rules; our moderation team reviews every report.
- Block anyone: you will stop seeing their content.
- By creating an account you accept the Terms and Conditions.
- You can delete your account and your data at any time from Settings.

Foreign is for higher-education students. Download the app and make your new city feel like home.
```
